Skuja Design Studio · Website redesign · Phase 2

Three directions for the new homepage

Each concept is a working homepage built with the studio's own photographs, shown live at desktop and phone size. Scroll inside any preview, open it full screen, and try the menu and the “Start a project” button. Nothing has been applied to the real site yet.

  1. 1Open each concept full screen on your computer and on your phone.
  2. 2Look at how your work is presented: does it feel like your furniture?
  3. 3Pick one direction. Mixing details (a font, a section) from another is fine.
Concept A

Organic Modern

Warm, soft and inviting, like walking into the workshop.

Full screen ↗
Desktop · 1440
Phone · 390

Design principles

  • Linen and walnut palette taken from the logo's olive and the wood itself
  • Arched and rounded photo frames echo hand-shaped edges
  • Tells the process: conversation, design, making, delivery
  • The inquiry starts right in the hero with project-type chips

Look and type

Fraunces, soft serifFigtree for reading text

Strengths

  • Most approachable; lowers the barrier to ask
  • Explains materials and pricing gently
  • Works with everyday phone photos

Tradeoffs

  • Less dramatic; work shares space with text
  • Warm-earthy look is common among makers

Best if

you want first-time clients to feel welcome and comfortable sharing a budget.

Design principles

  • Poster-scale condensed type over full-bleed photography
  • Strict 12-column grid used asymmetrically, like magazine spreads
  • A numbered project index with image previews on hover
  • Figure captions name materials, like a design publication

Look and type

Archivo CondensedNewsreader text, IBM Plex Mono captions

Strengths

  • Most memorable and distinctive
  • Signals design ambition to architects and designers
  • Index scales well as the portfolio grows

Tradeoffs

  • Needs strong landscape hero photos
  • Can feel less personal to homeowners

Best if

you want to attract architects, designers and larger commissions.

Approval

Choose one direction. Once approved, it becomes the visual system for the whole site: portfolio and project pages, saunas, journal, the guided inquiry, the shop (if switched on) and the emails customers receive.

Until then, the working site and owner dashboard use a neutral style, so no decision is locked in early.

What stays the same in every direction

Your existing page addresses (Home, Portfolio, Sauna, About, Contact), the custom sauna offering, your own photographs and words, and the six-step project inquiry with budget guidance you control from the dashboard.

Journal entries in the concepts are format examples. No prices or products are shown, and nothing is for sale in these mockups. Project names are working titles to confirm with the studio.
