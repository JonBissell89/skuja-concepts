How a piece comes to be

From a conversation to an heirloom

Every piece starts with how you live: the room, the people around the table, the way light falls. Then we choose the wood and build it to last.

  1. Conversation

    You tell us what you need, the space, and a comfortable budget. We listen first.

  2. Design

    Sketches, dimensions and timber options, refined together until it feels right.

  3. Making

    Built by hand in our Vermont workshop with traditional joinery and careful finishing.

  4. Delivery

    Delivered and installed with care, then cared for over the years ahead.

Materials

Solid hardwood, chosen board by board

We build with solid timber and honest joinery: dovetailed drawers, hand-shaped edges, natural oil finishes. Size, species and complexity shape the price more than anything else, and we will always explain the trade-offs.

About the studio
“Each piece is made to become a cherished heirloom, something your family lives with for generations.”

Skuja Design Studio is a small furniture workshop in Wilmington, Vermont. From dining tables to cabinets and built-in storage, every piece is designed around the people who will use it and made with the finest materials and traditional techniques.
