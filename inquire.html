    What budget feels comfortable?

    Every piece is made to order, so there’s no price list — but knowing your comfort zone helps us suggest the right size, wood and design.

    What shapes the price?
    • Size — bigger pieces need more material and more time.
    • Wood — species like walnut and white oak cost more than maple or ash, and wide, figured boards are rare.
    • Design & joinery — curves, drawers, doors and hand-cut joinery add hours at the bench.
    • Finish — oil, hardwax or lacquer each take different preparation.
    • Quantity — sets and repeats are efficient, but every piece is still made by hand.
    • Delivery & installation — distance, access and on-site fitting.
